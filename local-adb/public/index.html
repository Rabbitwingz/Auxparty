YT Music Remote

Connect your phone

  1. On the phone: Settings → Developer options → Wireless debugging, turn it on.
  2. Tap Pair device with pairing code. Keep that screen open.
  3. Enter the IP:port and 6-digit code it shows below.

Pairing is one-time. After that this reconnects on its own via mDNS, even when Android reshuffles the port.
